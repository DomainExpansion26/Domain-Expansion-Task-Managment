## 1. What Is Phase 6?

Phase 6 is the **Launch & Iterate** phase. In Phase 5, we built the monetization system, hardened security, and prepared for production. Phase 6 takes TechGuild from a finished product to a live, growing platform serving real members across India.

This phase has four major stages:

1. **Beta Launch** - invite 50 parties and 50 clients to use TechGuild in a controlled environment. Collect feedback, find bugs, and validate that the platform works end-to-end for real users.
2. **Iteration Cycles** - process beta feedback, fix issues, and refine the product. Run 2-week iteration sprints during the beta period.
3. **Public Launch** - open TechGuild to the public. Begin marketing and user acquisition. Monitor closely for the first 30 days.
4. **Continuous Improvement** - ongoing development based on user feedback, data analytics, and market signals. This stage has no end date.

**Phase 6 transforms TechGuild from a product into a business.**

Unlike Phases 1-5 which had fixed 8-week timelines, Phase 6 is ongoing. The beta launch has a defined timeline (8 weeks), but continuous improvement runs indefinitely.

## 7. Feedback Collection Strategy

### In-App Feedback Widget
- Accessible from every screen via floating button (bottom-left, not interfering with FAB)
- Three options: Report Bug, Request Feature, General Feedback
- Bug report: description, screenshot (auto-capture), screen URL, browser/device info
- Feature request: description, category, priority (member perspective)
- General feedback: free text, sentiment (positive/neutral/negative)
- All feedback routed to product team dashboard
- Response within 48 hours for bugs, 1 week for feature requests

### Structured Surveys

**Weekly Pulse Survey (during beta):**
- Sent every Monday to beta members
- 3 questions: "How was your experience this week?", "What frustrated you?", "What did you love?"
- Takes under 2 minutes to complete
- Target response rate: 70%+

**Post-Quest Survey:**
- Triggered when quest is completed
- 5 questions: satisfaction with platform, satisfaction with other party, payment experience, communication quality, open feedback
- Takes under 3 minutes
- Target response rate: 80%+

**Post-Withdrawal Survey:**
- Triggered when withdrawal is processed
- 3 questions: withdrawal speed satisfaction, ease of process, open feedback
- Takes under 1 minute
- Target response rate: 60%+

**NPS Survey:**
- Sent at 30 days after signup
- 1 question: "How likely are you to recommend TechGuild to a friend or colleague?" (0-10)
- Follow-up: "What is the main reason for your score?"
- Target: NPS above 30 during beta, above 40 post-launch

### User Interviews
- 5-10 interviews per week during beta (Weeks 1-8)
- 30 minutes each, conducted via video call
- Semi-structured: 5 standard questions + follow-ups
- Questions:
  1. "Walk me through your last experience on TechGuild."
  2. "What was the most frustrating part?"
  3. "What surprised you (positively or negatively)?"
  4. "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
  5. "Would you recommend TechGuild? Why or why not?"
- Notes and recordings (with consent) shared with product team
- Insights compiled weekly

### Session Recordings
- With explicit consent (opt-in during onboarding)
- Tool: Hotjar or FullStory
- Record: clicks, scrolls, form inputs (sensitive fields masked)
- Analysis: identify usability issues, drop-off points, confusion patterns
- Privacy: recordings not shared outside product team, deleted after 90 days

### Analytics Tracking
- Funnel analysis: signup -> onboarding -> first quest -> first payment
- Feature usage: which features are used most/least
- Drop-off analysis: where do members stop using the platform
- Retention analysis: daily, weekly, monthly active members
- Time-to-first-quest: how long from signup to first quest action
- Payment funnel: quest posted -> accepted -> completed -> paid -> withdrawn

## 8. Iteration Cycle Process

### Sprint Structure (2-Week Sprints During Beta)

**Sprint Planning (Day 1):**
- Product team reviews all feedback from previous sprint
- Prioritize: critical bugs (P0), high-impact improvements (P1), nice-to-haves (P2)
- Select items for sprint (capacity-based)
- Assign owners
- Define acceptance criteria

**Sprint Execution (Days 2-10):**
- Daily standup (15 minutes)
- Development and testing
- Can deploy to beta environment at any time (continuous deployment for beta)

**Sprint Review (Day 11):**
- Demo completed work to product team
- Collect feedback
- Decide what goes to beta members

**Sprint Deploy (Day 12):**
- Deploy to beta environment
- Notify beta members via in-app changelog and WhatsApp/Slack
- Monitor for issues (24-hour watch)

**Sprint Retro (Day 13-14):**
- What went well, what did not, what to improve
- Process improvements for next sprint

### Prioritization Framework
- **P0 (Critical):** Bugs that block core functionality, security issues, data loss
  - Fix within 24 hours, deploy immediately
- **P1 (High):** Bugs that affect significant user flow, high-impact improvements
  - Fix within sprint (2 weeks)
- **P2 (Medium):** Minor bugs, moderate improvements, feature requests
  - Plan for next sprint or public launch
- **P3 (Low):** Cosmetic issues, minor enhancements
  - Backlog, address when capacity allows

### A/B Testing During Beta
- For major changes (UI redesigns, flow changes), A/B test with beta members
- Split: 50% control (current), 50% treatment (new)
- Metrics: task completion rate, time on task, error rate, satisfaction
- Duration: 1 week minimum
- Decision: ship if treatment is statistically better, otherwise iterate or abandon

### Communication with Beta Members
- Weekly update (Monday): what changed last week, what is coming this week
- In-app changelog: accessible from dashboard, shows all changes
- WhatsApp/Slack channel: real-time communication, quick responses
- Direct email for important announcements
- Beta member survey: feedback on changes (did this help?)

## 10. Continuous Improvement Process

### Monthly Release Cycle
- **Week 1:** Release deployment (new features, improvements, bug fixes)
  - Canary deployment (5% -> 25% -> 50% -> 100%)
  - Monitor for 48 hours after full deployment
  - Rollback if issues detected
- **Week 2:** Hotfixes for any release issues
- **Week 3:** Development for next release
- **Week 4:** Testing and staging for next release

### Monthly Product Roadmap Review
- Review data: GMV, revenue, retention, churn, feature usage, NPS
- Review feedback: user feedback, support tickets, feature requests
- Prioritize: what to build next based on data and feedback
- Plan: monthly release scope
- Communicate: roadmap shared with members (public roadmap page)

### Quarterly Business Review
- **Metrics review:**
  - GMV and growth rate
  - Revenue (subscription + transaction fees)
  - Member growth (parties, clients)
  - Retention (30-day, 90-day)
  - Churn rate and reasons
  - NPS and CSAT
  - Feature adoption rates
- **Financial review:**
  - Revenue vs. expenses
  - Unit economics (CAC, LTV, payback period)
  - Runway and funding needs
- **Strategic review:**
  - Market position
  - Competitive landscape
  - Growth opportunities
  - Risks and mitigations
- **Output:** updated roadmap, adjusted targets, resource allocation

### Experimentation Framework
- All major changes go through A/B testing
- Hypothesis: what we expect to happen
- Metric: what we measure
- Duration: minimum 2 weeks
- Sample size: calculated for statistical significance
- Decision: ship if positive, iterate if neutral, abandon if negative
- Documentation: all experiments documented with results

## 16. Questions for Team Discussion

1. **Beta recruitment** - How do we find 50 parties and 50 clients in 2 weeks? What is our primary channel? Do we offer incentives beyond free Pro access?

2. **Beta duration** - 8 weeks for beta. Is that enough? Some products beta for 3-6 months. Too short risks launching with bugs. Too long delays revenue.

3. **Public launch timing** - Do we launch right after beta (Week 9), or take a 2-week buffer to fix remaining issues? Buffer is safer but loses momentum.

4. **Marketing budget** - Rs 2,00,000 per month for paid ads. Is that enough for Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi? Should we concentrate on one city first?

5. **Pricing validation** - Beta is the first time real users see our pricing (Rs 999/month Pro). What if beta feedback says it is too expensive? Do we adjust before public launch?

6. **Support scaling** - During beta, product team handles support directly. At public launch, how do we scale? Hire support agents? Use AI chatbot for first response?

7. **Geographic expansion priority** - India Tier 2 cities or Southeast Asia first? Tier 2 is lower risk (same country, same compliance) but smaller market. SEA is bigger but more complex.

8. **Mobile app timing** - Beta members will ask for a mobile app. Do we build one in Phase 6 (stretching resources) or wait for Phase 7? PWA is a middle ground.

9. **Public API timing** - Some clients may want API access for integration. Do we open the API in Phase 6 or wait? Opening early creates support burden. Waiting loses enterprise clients.

10. **Churn management** - What is our acceptable churn rate? 10% monthly means we lose over 70% of members in a year. Is that sustainable? What retention strategies do we deploy first?

## 17. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Beta members find critical bugs not caught in testing | High | High | 4 iteration sprints to fix, delay public launch if P0 bugs remain |
| Public launch gets no traction (low signups) | Medium | High | Marketing budget, content marketing, community building, referrals |
| Payment gateway issues at scale | Medium | High | Dual gateway (Razorpay + Stripe), monitoring, fallback |
| Negative press or reviews | Medium | Medium | Monitor sentiment, respond quickly, address issues transparently |
| Churn higher than expected | High | High | Onboarding optimization, retention emails, feature improvements, exit surveys |
| Security incident at launch | Low | Critical | Security hardening from Phase 5, monitoring, incident response plan |
| Infrastructure cannot handle public traffic | Medium | High | Load testing, auto-scaling, CDN, monitoring |
| Competition launches similar feature | Medium | Medium | Speed of execution, community moat (Guild Hall), verified reputation moat |
| Tax compliance issues | Low | High | CA review, automated compliance checks, legal consultation |
| Team burnout during launch | High | Medium | Sustainable pace, hiring, clear roles and responsibilities |
